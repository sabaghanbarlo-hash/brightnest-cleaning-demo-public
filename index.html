Frequently asked questions

Sample answers for a demo company. A real business would adjust these to match its policies.

What should I do to prepare for a cleaning?

Picking up personal items from floors and counters helps the cleaners reach more surfaces. Let the team know about anything delicate or off-limits.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

That is usually up to you. Some people stay, others arrange access in advance. A real company would explain its own access policy here.

Do you bring your own supplies?

In this example, yes, the team brings standard supplies. If you prefer specific products, mention it in your quote request.

How do I get a price?

Use the quote form on this page. Pricing in real life depends on home size, condition, and the type of service, so no prices are listed in this demo.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

A real company would state its rescheduling and cancellation terms here. This demo does not take real bookings.
